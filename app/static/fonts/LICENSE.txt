DejaVu Sans (DejaVuSans.ttf, DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf)

Bundled here so chart images in exported reports render consistently
regardless of what fonts are installed on the machine running the app
(this matters because the app may run on Windows, where the Linux/macOS
system font paths this code would otherwise look for don't exist).

DejaVu fonts are a derivative of the Bitstream Vera fonts. The license is
permissive and explicitly allows redistribution, modification, and
